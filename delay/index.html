Studio / FX

Delay and space effect.Echo and depth in VibeDeck Studio

There is no separate delay knob in VibeDeck Studio. Instead, the space effect gives you echo and room: it adds depth and movement to tracks and loops, with a soft reverb tail. Turn it up on a loop, record up to two minutes and share the clip. Works offline on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Download on theApp StoreGet it onGoogle Play

A delay repeats the signal after a short gap, which the ear hears as echo. Space in VibeDeck Studio gives you that echo-like feel plus a soft reverb tail, so a loop sounds bigger than the room it was recorded in.

Features / 03

Echo without the knob

  • 01

    Space covers delay

    No dedicated delay control: the space effect brings the echo-like depth and soft reverb tail directly.

  • 02

    Made for loops

    Runs on beat-snapped loops from half a bar to 8 bars, tuned to the grid, where echo matters most.

  • 03

    Record and share

    Capture up to two minutes with fade in and out, and the space is baked into the clip you share.

How to use it

  1. Install VibeDeck Player from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open any local track in VibeDeck Studio.
  3. Start a beat-snapped loop over the part you want.
  4. Switch on the space effect and dial it in by ear: a little adds air, more adds depth and tail.
  5. Record up to two minutes and share the result.

Who uses it

  • DJs giving loops a bigger, deeper sound before recording.
  • Producers sketching atmospheres on a loop.
  • Slowed + reverb makers who want space and tail in one effect.
  • Anyone who likes how a track sounds with a little room around it.

Also see: saturation effect, DJ filter effect.

FAQ / 05

Delay and space: FAQ

Is there a delay knob in VibeDeck Player?

No. There is no separate delay knob in the release. The Studio space effect gives you echo and room instead, which covers the same use case for loops.

What does the space effect do?

Space adds echo-like depth and a soft reverb tail to the track or loop you are playing, so it sounds bigger and more atmospheric.

Does space work on loops?

Yes. Space runs on Studio loops from half a bar to 8 bars, snapped to the beat grid.

Can I record with the space effect?

Yes. Studio records up to two minutes with fade in and out, and the effect is captured in the clip, ready to share.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Studio effects, including space, work offline on files stored on your device.
