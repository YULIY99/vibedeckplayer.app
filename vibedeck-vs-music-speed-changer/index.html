VibeDeck vs Music Speed Changer. Two speed changers, compared on facts

VibeDeck Player and Music Speed Changer both change the speed and pitch of your own music. This page compares them from their public store listings and websites: platforms, features and file sources. It lists facts only, with no ratings and no rankings.

Several apps use the name Music Speed Changer. This page means the one by Single Minded Productions, described on musicspeedchanger.com. Both apps work with files you own, so the real question is which tools you need around the speed change.

Features / 03

Same job, different extras

  • 01

    What both do

    Change tempo and pitch independently, loop a section, use an equalizer, add effects and split a song into parts with on-device stem separation.

  • 02

    Where Music Speed Changer goes further

    Its listings name Mac and web versions, a Chrome extension, markers, key detection and stem separation into more parts.

  • 03

    Where VibeDeck goes further

    No ads, no account and no tracking, a Sampler that records the sound playing on your phone, a 3-band DJ EQ next to the 8-band equalizer, Studio and the AURA visualizer.

Which one to pick

Pick Music Speed Changer if you need a Mac or web version, a Chrome extension, markers or stem separation into more parts. Pick VibeDeck Player if you want an offline player with no ads, no account and no tracking, a recorder for the sound playing on your phone, a DJ-style EQ, loops and effects in Studio, and the same app on iPhone, iPad and Android.

FAQ / 06

VibeDeck vs Music Speed Changer: FAQ

Is VibeDeck Player an alternative to Music Speed Changer?

Both change the speed and pitch of your own music. VibeDeck Player is a full offline music player with no ads, no account and no tracking, plus Studio, a Sampler and Stems.

Can both change pitch without changing speed?

Yes. Both change tempo and pitch independently.

Which one has stem separation?

Both. VibeDeck Stems splits a song into vocal and instrumental on the device. Music Speed Changer lists separation into more parts.

Which one can record sound from other apps?

VibeDeck Player has a Sampler that records the sound playing on your phone, from YouTube, TikTok and many other apps; protected playback cannot be recorded. The Music Speed Changer listing does not mention recording.

Which platforms does each one cover?

VibeDeck Player runs on iPhone, iPad and Android. Music Speed Changer lists iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, a web app and a Chrome extension.

Does this page rate the two apps?

No. It lists public facts only, with no ratings and no rankings.
