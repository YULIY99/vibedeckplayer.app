VibeDeck Support

Help with VibeDeck, an offline music player for local files on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

For support, questions, or bug reports, contact hello@vibedeckplayer.app.

Importing Tracks

VibeDeck imports local MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A (including ALAC), AAC, AIFF/AIF and CAF files you choose from iOS Files, iCloud Drive, Downloads, AirDrop, and supported share sources. No WiFi is needed for offline playback.

Queue And Playback

The queue helps prepare DJ-style sets, review current and upcoming tracks, search local library items, and keep playback context visible.

Sound Tools

VibeDeck includes pitch shifter and tempo control, a 3-band equalizer with limiter, VibeMod and Tone modes, waveform display, bass booster, and transport controls.
