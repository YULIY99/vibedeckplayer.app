VibeDeck Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Overview

VibeDeck is an offline music player and audio control app for iOS and Android. It is used for importing, organizing, analyzing, and playing local audio files on your device, with no account required.

Personal Data

VibeDeck does not require an account, include ads, or sell personal information. The app is intended to work with local audio files that you choose to import.

Audio Files

Audio files and track metadata are used to provide playback, waveform, queue, analysis, pitch, filter, VibeMod, and equalizer features. Imported files stay on the device unless you export, move, or share them using the operating system. Your audio files are not uploaded to VibeDeck and are not sent to Apple iTunes Search or Discogs.

Internet And Cover Art

VibeDeck can use the internet to look up missing cover art. When local or embedded artwork is not available, or when you refresh a cover, the app may send the track’s artist and title as a search query to Apple iTunes Search and, if needed, Discogs. If a cover image URL is returned, the app may download that image. The audio file itself is not sent to those services. This lookup is optional; playback works without it.

Purchases

Optional Premium subscriptions and purchases are processed by the Apple App Store on iOS and by Google Play Billing on Android. VibeDeck does not handle payment card details.

Analytics, Advertising, And Tracking

VibeDeck does not include a third-party advertising SDK, and it does not include its own analytics or Firebase. The app does not sell user data. If analytics or crash reporting tools are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before release.

Links

In-app Terms and Privacy links may open in the device’s external browser.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, the app, feature requests, or anything else, contact hello@vibedeckplayer.app.
