Feature / Sampler

Capture any moment with the Sampler. Record, trim, loop

The VibeDeck Sampler captures audio playing on your device, lets you trim the part you want and turns it into a loop. You can then shape that loop in VibeDeck Studio. It is part of the VibeDeck Player update and works with no account and no ads.

A great moment in a song is short: a drum fill, a vocal line, a single chord. The Sampler lets you grab it while it plays, cut it to the exact length and keep it as a loop instead of searching for it again later.

How it works

  1. Open VibeDeck Player and go to the Sampler.
  2. Capture the moment while the audio plays.
  3. Trim the capture to the part you want.
  4. Turn it into a loop and open it in Studio to shape it.

Good to know

  • Your library: the Sampler cards sit in the Library next to Studio.
  • Offline: VibeDeck is an offline player, so captures stay on your device.
  • Studio: loops from half a bar to 8 bars, pitch, filter, saturation, space and presets, and a clip recorder, on iPhone and iPad.

Only capture audio you have the right to use. Samples of other people’s music may be protected by copyright.

FAQ / 05

VibeDeck Sampler: FAQ

What is the VibeDeck Sampler?

The Sampler captures audio playing on your device and lets you trim it. You can turn the result into a loop and shape it in VibeDeck Studio.

Can I trim what I capture?

Yes. After capturing, you cut the recording to the part you want to keep.

Where do I shape the loop?

In VibeDeck Studio, which is part of VibeDeck Player on iPhone and iPad.

Do I need an account?

No. VibeDeck Player needs no account and shows no ads.

Is it free?

VibeDeck Player is free to download. New users also get 48 hours of VibeDeck PRO as a welcome gift, with everything unlocked and no card needed.
