Feature / Sampler

Record any sound playing on your phone. Press Rec. Play anything. Keep it

The VibeDeck Sampler records the sound playing on your phone, straight from the system. Press Rec, switch to YouTube, SoundCloud, TikTok or most other apps, and come back when you have what you need. No microphone, no room noise, no computer. On iPhone and Android.

You hear something great in a video, a stream or a live set, and there is no download button. Holding another phone to the speaker sounds terrible. The Sampler records the sound straight from your phone, clean, while it plays.

Features / 03

Hear it, press Rec, keep it

  • 01

    Any app

    YouTube, SoundCloud, TikTok, a browser, a game, a voice message. Most apps that play sound on your phone can be recorded.

  • 02

    Clean system audio

    MP3, AAC or WAV up to 320 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo, straight from the phone. A live meter shows the sound coming in.

  • 03

    Edit on the waveform

    The recording turns into a waveform. Set the in and out points, add a fade in and out of 0.5, 1 or 2 seconds and preview it.

How to record sound from your phone

  1. Install VibeDeck Player from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open VibeDeck and go to Sampler.
  3. Press Rec.
  4. Play the sound in YouTube, SoundCloud or any other app.
  5. Come back and press Stop. The sample appears in Recorded Samples.
  6. Tap it to edit it on the waveform.

What to do with the recording

  • Share: send it to any app.
  • Save to Library: it becomes a track in All Tracks.
  • Open in Studio: loop it, bend it with pitch, filter, saturation and space, record your version and share it.

What people record

  • A moment from a video: a beat, a line, a sound effect.
  • A live stream or DJ set: the part you want to hear again.
  • Raw material for your own sound: record it, twist it in Studio and post the result.
  • Practice material: a riff or a vocal line to learn, then slow it down or change the key.

Every new user gets 48 hours with everything unlocked, with no card and no sign-up. After that the free version plays music, also in the background. The Sampler, Studio, pitch and the other tools need VibeDeck PRO.

Record only what you have the right to use. Music and videos made by other people are usually protected by copyright.

FAQ / 07

VibeDeck Sampler: FAQ

How do I record internal audio on my phone?

Open the VibeDeck Sampler, press Rec and play the sound in any app. VibeDeck records the system audio while it plays.

Can I record sound from YouTube?

Yes. Start recording in VibeDeck, then play the video in YouTube. The sound playing on your phone is recorded.

Does it use the microphone?

No. It records the system audio, so there is no room noise in the recording. Microphone and video are not saved.

What quality is the recording?

Choose MP3 for any device, AAC for smaller files or WAV for uncompressed sound. Bitrate from 128 to 320 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. The Sampler works the same way on iPhone and Android.

Can I record Apple Music?

No. Music protected by streaming services such as Apple Music is blocked by the system and records as silence. YouTube, SoundCloud and TikTok record fine.

Is it free?

Every new user gets 48 hours with everything unlocked, with no card and no sign-up. After that the free version plays music, also in the background. The Sampler, Studio, pitch and the other tools need VibeDeck PRO.
