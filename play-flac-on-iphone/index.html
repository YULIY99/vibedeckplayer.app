How-to / FLAC

How to play FLAC files on iPhone. Copy, import, play. No conversion

To play FLAC on iPhone, copy the files to your phone with AirDrop, iCloud Drive or the Files app, import them into VibeDeck Player and press play. There is nothing to convert, no internet is needed and there are no ads. The same steps work on iPad.

The Music app on iPhone does not add FLAC files to your library, so many guides tell you to convert everything first. You do not have to. A player that opens FLAC directly keeps your lossless files exactly as they are and still gives you an EQ, pitch and tempo.

Features / 03

FLAC on iPhone, the easy way

  • 01

    No conversion

    VibeDeck Player opens FLAC as it is. Your lossless files keep every detail, and you keep one copy instead of two.

  • 02

    Any way to copy

    AirDrop from a Mac, iCloud Drive from any computer, or any place the Files app can reach.

  • 03

    Sound controls

    EQ with gain, bass boost, filter, stereo balance and a limiter, plus pitch and tempo.

Step by step

  1. Install VibeDeck Player from the App Store.
  2. Copy your FLAC files to the iPhone. From a Mac, select them and send them with AirDrop. From a Windows PC, upload them to iCloud Drive. Any place the Files app shows works too.
  3. Open VibeDeck Player and import the files from Files, iCloud Drive or Downloads.
  4. Press play. The music plays offline, with no internet.
  5. Shape the sound with the EQ, or change pitch and tempo if you practice along.

Good to know

  • Storage: FLAC files are bigger than MP3, so check free space before you copy a large collection.
  • Mixed library: MP3 and WAV play in the same queue as your FLAC files.
  • Streaming tracks: songs inside streaming apps cannot be opened by other apps. VibeDeck plays files you own.

The core player is free with no ads. VibeDeck Premium unlocks the full sound engine.

FAQ / 05

Playing FLAC on iPhone: FAQ

Can iPhone play FLAC files?

Yes. iPhone can open FLAC files, but the Music app does not add them to your library. VibeDeck Player imports FLAC files and plays them offline with an EQ, pitch and tempo control.

Do I need to convert FLAC to play it on iPhone?

No. VibeDeck Player plays FLAC directly, so there is nothing to convert and no quality is lost.

How do I get FLAC files onto my iPhone without a cable?

Use AirDrop from a Mac, or upload the files to iCloud Drive from any computer. Then import them into VibeDeck Player from the Files app or iCloud Drive.

Does FLAC playback work offline?

Yes. Once imported, the files are on your iPhone and play without WiFi or mobile data.

Is it free?

The core player is free and has no ads. VibeDeck Premium is optional and unlocks the full sound engine: pitch and filter, EQ with gain, limiter, VibeMod, TONE, AURA and Smart Metadata.
