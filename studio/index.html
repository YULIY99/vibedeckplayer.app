What’s new / 03

Everything in Studio

  • 01

    Loops on the grid

    Loop ½ to 8 bars, snapped to the beat grid. Fine-tune the grid and the loop start and end until it sits perfectly.

  • 02

    Shape the sound

    Pitch, filter, saturation and space, plus sound presets to get a vibe in one tap. Everything reacts live while the loop plays.

  • 03

    REC and share

    Record your live session for up to 2 minutes with automatic fade in and out, then share it as a clip.

How it works

  1. Open a track from your library in VibeDeck Studio.
  2. Pick a loop length from ½ to 8 bars. It snaps to the beat grid; nudge the grid or the loop edges if needed.
  3. Shape it live with pitch, filter, saturation and space, or start from a sound preset.
  4. Press REC, perform up to 2 minutes, and share the clip.

FAQ / 05

VibeDeck Studio FAQ

What is VibeDeck Studio?

VibeDeck Studio is the remix and clip section of VibeDeck Player. Loop any part of a track from ½ to 8 bars on the beat grid, shape it with pitch, filter, saturation, space and sound presets, then record your live session and share it as a clip.

How long can a recorded clip be?

Press REC to capture your live session for up to 2 minutes. Fade in and fade out are applied automatically, and the clip is ready to share.

How do I make a slowed + reverb edit on my phone?

Open the song in VibeDeck Studio, lower the pitch, turn up Space for reverb, and press REC to capture the result as a clip.

Does Studio keep playing in the background?

Yes. Studio keeps playing when you lock the screen or switch apps.

Is VibeDeck Studio available now?

Yes. Studio is included in the latest update of VibeDeck Player. Update the app to get it.
