Updates / 1.0.21

What is new in VibeDeck Player. Version 1.0.21

VibeDeck Player 1.0.21 brings Stems: split any track into acapella and backing, mix them live, or open them in Studio and add any effects, then export. VibeDeck now fits every iPad and every iPhone, old and new. Found covers now show on the lock screen too, and you can search a cover for any track.

Every update of VibeDeck comes from the same idea: same track, new feeling. Version 1.0.21 adds the biggest tool so far, Stems, and makes the app fit every screen, from the smallest iPhone to the biggest iPad.

Version 1.0.21

  • Stems: split any track into acapella and backing, right on your phone. Mix them live, or switch to Studio and add any effects. Export the result.
  • iPad: VibeDeck now fits every iPad and every iPhone, old and new.
  • Covers: found covers now show on the lock screen too. Search a cover for any track.
  • Bug fixes and improvements.

Version 1.0.20

  • Share many tracks at once: select a batch of your own tracks, share them to VibeDeck and keep going. They will be in your queue when you get back.
  • Sort by BPM: the queue groups by tempo range, with the exact BPM on every track.
  • Pick your waveform: choose how the now-playing track moves, Needles or Bars.
  • Stems has its own home: find it in Library, under Tools.
  • Share from the queue: send the track you are playing straight from its menu.
  • Locked to the beat: NEXT and the EQ lamp move with your music.
  • A brighter Hub, a smoother queue, smoother Studio knobs, and bug fixes.

What VibeDeck can do

  • Player: MP3, AAC, M4A, WAV, AIFF and FLAC offline, with pitch, filter and BPM on the main screen.
  • Sound tools: 3-band DJ EQ or 8-band EQ from 60 Hz to 16 kHz, VibeMod, limiter and mono.
  • Effects: one-tap presets Slowed + Reverb, Nightcore, Dub Delay and Vinyl Drive, plus bass boost, saturation, reverb and echo.
  • Studio: classic DJ pitch, master tempo, beat-synced loops from half a bar to 8 bars, REC.
  • Sampler: record any sound playing on your phone, trim it with fade in and fade out, share it.
  • Queue: drag to reorder, change any cover, night mode.

Every new user gets 48 hours with everything unlocked, with no card and no sign-up. After that the free version plays music, also in the background. The effects, the EQ and the other sound tools need VibeDeck PRO.

Also see: vocal remover and stem splitter, nightcore on your phone, BPM of every song.

FAQ / 05

VibeDeck updates: FAQ

What is new in VibeDeck Player 1.0.21?

Stems splits any track into acapella and backing, VibeDeck now fits every iPad and iPhone, and found covers show on the lock screen too.

Does VibeDeck work on iPad?

Yes. VibeDeck fits every iPad and every iPhone, old and new.

Can VibeDeck remove vocals from a song?

Yes. Stems splits a track into acapella and backing on your phone. You can mix them live, add effects in Studio and export.

Can I change the cover of a track?

Yes. Search a cover for any track, pick one from Photos or keep the original. Found covers also show on the lock screen.

How do I get the update?

Update VibeDeck Player in the App Store. New users can download it for free.
