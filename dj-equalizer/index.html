Feature / DJ EQ

Switch between a 3-band DJ EQ and an 8-band equalizer. One tap. Same track. On iPhone

VibeDeck Player has two equalizers on one screen. The 3-band DJ EQ gives you big faders for gain, bass, mid and high, the way a DJ mixer does. The 8-band EQ shapes the sound in detail at 60, 150 and 400 Hz and at 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 kHz. Tap 3 BAND or 8 BAND to switch while the music plays.

DJs think in three moves: low, mid, high. Sound lovers want finer control: a little more air at 16 kHz, a little less boom at 150 Hz. Most players give you one or the other. VibeDeck gives you both and switches between them in one tap.

Features / 03

Two equalizers, one tap apart

  • 01

    3-band DJ EQ

    Gain, bass, mid and high on big vertical faders, from +12 down to -24 dB. Fast moves you can make without looking.

  • 02

    8-band equalizer

    Eight faders at 60, 150, 400, 1k, 2k, 4k, 8k and 16k, each from -12 to +12 dB, plus its own gain bar.

  • 03

    VibeMod, limiter, mono

    VibeMod adds dense, punchy processing. The limiter keeps loud peaks clean. Mono folds the mix to one channel, so producers can check that the kick and bass sit in the middle.

How to switch between the 3-band and 8-band EQ

  1. Install VibeDeck Player from the App Store.
  2. Play a track from your library and open the EQ.
  3. Tap 3 BAND for the DJ EQ and move GAIN, BASS, MID and HIGH.
  4. Tap 8 BAND for the 8-band equalizer and shape each band from 60 Hz to 16 kHz.
  5. Pushing the sound hard? Switch on LIMITER to keep the peaks clean. Tap VIBEMOD for a denser, punchier sound.
  6. Tap MONO to hear the mix in mono, and L/R to set the balance.
  7. Tap RESET EQ to go back to flat.

Good to know

  • Switch any time: the music keeps playing when you change from 3 bands to 8 and back.
  • Two styles of control: the 3-band DJ EQ is for quick, big moves. The 8-band EQ is for fine tuning to your headphones, speaker or car.
  • Your own files: MP3, FLAC, WAV and the other formats VibeDeck plays, offline.
  • On iPhone: the 3 BAND / 8 BAND switch is in the iPhone app.

Who uses it

  • Producers: play your own track in mono to check that the kick and bass sit in the center, hear phase problems and test how the mix sounds on a club system or a phone speaker. Then switch back to stereo and compare.
  • DJs: prepare tracks with the DJ EQ: cut the bass before a drop, lift the highs, check the mix on headphones.
  • Bass lovers: push 60 Hz for the sub and keep 150 Hz under control.
  • Headphone listeners: tune the 8 bands to the sound of your headphones.
  • FLAC collectors: fine EQ for lossless files.
  • Drivers: match the sound to the car speakers.

FAQ / 09

VibeDeck DJ EQ: FAQ

Does VibeDeck have a DJ EQ?

Yes. The 3-band DJ EQ has gain, bass, mid and high faders, like the EQ on a DJ mixer.

Does VibeDeck have an 8-band equalizer?

Yes, on iPhone. It has bands at 60 Hz, 150 Hz, 400 Hz, 1 kHz, 2 kHz, 4 kHz, 8 kHz and 16 kHz, each from -12 to +12 dB, and a separate gain control.

How do I switch between 3 bands and 8 bands?

Open the EQ and tap 3 BAND or 8 BAND at the top of the panel. The music keeps playing.

Which one should I use?

The 3-band DJ EQ for quick, DJ-style moves on bass, mids and highs. The 8-band EQ when you want to tune the sound in detail to your headphones, speaker or car.

Can I listen to a track in mono?

Yes. Tap MONO under the EQ. Producers use it to check that the kick and bass sit in the middle of the mix and that nothing disappears in mono.

What does VibeMod do?

VibeMod is a special dense processing of the sound. It makes the track fuller and punchier. Switch it on and off under the EQ to compare.

Will a big boost distort the sound?

A strong boost can make loud peaks clip. Switch on LIMITER to keep them clean.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The EQ works on the music files in your VibeDeck library, with no internet.

Is it free?

Every new user gets 48 hours with everything unlocked, with no card and no sign-up. After that the free version plays music, also in the background. The EQ, the limiter and the other sound tools need VibeDeck PRO.
